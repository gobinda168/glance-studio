# Security

Please report vulnerabilities privately, through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on this repository's
Security tab. Don't open a public issue.

Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and which version you tested (Glance Studio → About). You'll get a reply
within a week. Fixes ship in the next release, and you'll be credited in its notes unless you'd rather not be.

Glance Studio runs on your Mac with access to your terminals, your Claude Code sessions and, with your permission,
your microphone and Accessibility. Reports about any of these, the secrets broker (`Sources/GlanceCore/Secrets`), the
local hook server or the update feed are especially welcome.
