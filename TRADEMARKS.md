## What you may do without asking

- Say truthfully that your project is "based on Glance Studio" or "a fork of Glance Studio".
- Use the names to refer to the official app, for example in articles, tutorials, reviews or comparisons.
- Build and run the code for yourself, unmodified, under its own name.

## What needs a different name and icon

If you distribute a modified version (a fork, a repackaged build, a hosted service built on this code), it must:

- use a different name, one that isn't confusingly similar to Glance;
- replace the app icon and the orb logo (`assets/logo`, the app icon sets);
- use its own bundle identifier, update feed and signing keys, never the official ones;
- not suggest it is made, endorsed or supported by the Glance project.

## Official builds

Official builds are signed by the maintainer and published at the project's releases page. Only official builds use
the official update feed.

For anything not covered here, open an issue and ask.
